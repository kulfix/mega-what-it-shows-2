The problems

parser: every date is read month first

Reports: 379. Ticket: #1. The shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date whose day is 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: a booking for 7 April lands on 4 July. This is what #4471 describes, and its queued fix is the fix for this problem. Most of these reports show the swap in their own dates; a few only say the day was wrong, and land here by their tenant and channel.

hint: booking-v3 asks for the month first

Reports: 33. Ticket: #2. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, for tenants who write the day first. People type what the hint asks; the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The result looks the same as parser, but the parser fix does not touch it: the hint has to follow the tenant’s locale. Only reports sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant land here.

one-day: the date moves by one day

Reports: 5. Ticket: #3.

Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting. We held it twice.

These reports describe a date that is one day off, or an invite that arrived before its booking was made, never a swapped month. The cause is not known yet. Only the Lisbon/Toronto report points at time zones; a date stored as a moment in time and shown in another zone would do this, but that is a guess until someone reproduces it. It needs its own reproduction and its own fix.

Lessons

  • A subject line says how a report feels, not what broke. Different bugs share “wrong day”.
  • Before calling a merged ticket known, read what its own table shows: here, a row marked “no ticket of its own” and a footnote.
  • Notes the desk already wrote (here: the one-day moves) are evidence. Read them before merging.
